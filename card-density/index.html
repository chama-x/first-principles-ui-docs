Why & evidence

Caps the number of leaf text/icon elements inside sibling summary cards proportional to each card’s pixel area and row share (3–4 items for compact cards, up to 9 for anchor cards).

Why this works

Keep compact summary cards to at most 3–4 data points: label, value, and trend.

Atoms K <= 4 on compact card surfaces

Dumping 9 competing metrics onto a compact card overloads short-term working memory. Cap visual items at <= 4 discrete chunks to obey human subitizing limits, grouping secondary stats behind progressive disclosure.

Dashboard summary cards are meant to communicate state instantly. When a single card is packed with 10 different lines of metrics, dates, and decorative icons, pre-attentive comprehension collapses and users must consciously scan each item.

The substrate foundation is the psychophysics of Pre-Attentive Subitizing:

  1. Subitizing Range: Human vision can ascertain the number of items in a visual field instantaneously without sequential counting, but only up to 4 items (Cowan's working memory capacity $4 \pm 1$).
  2. Beyond 4 Items: When a summary card presents 5 or more distinct data atoms, processing switches to slow sequential counting (+250ms per item).
  3. Anchor vs Summary Roles: Large flagship cards can house up to 9 atoms, but compact 4-column cards must strictly observe the 3–4 atom limit.

Remediation

Distill compact cards down to 3 high-impact signals: metric label, primary figure, and delta indicator:

// Evaluated by src/rules/card-density.mjs
const atoms = inspectCardAtoms(card);
const maxAtoms = isLargeAnchorCard ? 9 : 4;

if (atoms.totalAtoms > maxAtoms) {
  ctx.report("LAW_02_SUBITIZING_OVERLOAD", {
    property: "leaf_atom_count",
    computed: atoms.totalAtoms,
    threshold: maxAtoms,
  });
}

What to Look For & How the Linter Fixes It

Visual signs in your UI and how the verifier guides your coding agent.

Visual Symptom
  • A single dashboard card contains 8 distinct metrics, dates, and badges, forcing users to squint and parse.
  • Cards look cluttered and overwhelming rather than providing at-a-glance insight.
  • Users miss critical alerts because they are buried alongside secondary metadata.
Linter Check
  • Compact summary card leaf atom count > 4 (or anchor card > 9).
  • Forward Oracle: ./check-ui-math.mjs calc density --atoms 7 --width 320

Agent Prompts & Oracles

Plug directly into Claude, Codex, or terminal execution.

Build a set of compact metric summary cards for an enterprise analytics dashboard. Limit each card to exactly 3 data atoms: a clear uppercase label, a prominent primary figure, and an actionable delta indicator. Follow pre-attentive the 4-item glance limit (K <= 4 atoms).
