Stage 5: Physical Ergonomics

Make Gestures, Sheets & Popovers Interruptible

Build physically grounded gesture sheets and popovers with 1:1 pointer tracking, velocity-seeded spring releases, critical damping, and interruptible state machines.

Full reference & code

Animations that lock out touch input break direct physical manipulation and make apps feel unresponsive. Keep all gesture interactions interruptible with momentum-preserving velocity handoff (v_exit = v_gesture), letting users redirect in flight.

Gesture Momentum & Direct Tracking

Interactive Drills

When users touch a digital surface, they expect Newtonian physics: objects track fingers with zero latency, retain velocity upon release, and can be caught mid-flight without locking input.

Two-Phase Direct Manipulation

LAW_30_SPRING_INTERRUPTIBILITY models interactive sheets and cards as two distinct physical phases:

  1. Phase 1: Direct Tracking (1:1 Drag): The element tracks pointer translation directly with zero spring lag. Damping and stiffness do not apply during active drag.
  2. Phase 2: Velocity-Seeded Spring Release: When released, the solver projects pointer velocity ($v_0$) into a second-order harmonic oscillator: m * x'' + c * x' + k * x = 0

Solving this differential equation with critical damping (zeta = c / (2 * sqrt(m * k)) = 1.0) ensures the element settles at its target boundary in minimum time without underdamped bouncing or sluggish overdamping.

What to Look For & How the Linter Fixes It

Visual signs in your UI and how the verifier guides your coding agent.

Visual Symptom
  • A modal or drawer locks user touch input while an animation is playing.
  • Swiping a bottom sheet feels disconnected because the sheet animates on a fixed timer rather than tracking the finger 1:1.
  • Flicking a drawer causes it to snap abruptly instead of projecting momentum.
  • Popovers scale up from the center of the screen instead of expanding from the trigger button.
Linter Check
  • Pointer event listeners disabled or pointer-events: none locked during gesture transitions.
  • Spring damping ratio zeta < 0.80 or > 1.20 for productive UI.
  • Forward Oracle: ./check-ui-math.mjs calc spring --stiffness 280 --damping 32 --mass 1 --velocity 1200
