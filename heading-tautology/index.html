Why & evidence

Emits HARD_ERROR when an identical badge text, icon signature, or CTA link label repeats on > 25% of sibling cards (N >= 3), and SOFT_WARN when element-type frequency exceeds 25% with varied values.

These are teaching examples, not live verifier readings. Project thresholds are not all universal standards.

Why this works

Delete badges that repeat the card title; show a live count or status change instead.

Shannon Entropy H(Body | Heading) >= 2.4 bits

Placing an 'Active' badge next to an 'Active Projects' heading wastes user visual bandwidth with zero new entropy. Purge duplicate words and replace repetitive pills with contextual data so every token delivers unique information.

  1. Zero Information Gain: If a section header reads "Active Users", placing a status pill that says "Active" right beside it yields mutual information I(H; B) ≈ H(B). The pill delivers zero new information (0 bits).
  2. Repetitive Garnish: If 4 out of 4 cards in a summary grid carry the exact same "Status: Live" pill, the pill ceases to function as a status indicator and becomes visual noise.
  3. The 4-Rung Subtractive Copy Ladder:
    • Rung 1 (Cut Unprompted Worry-Planting): Delete reassuring copy explaining things users never doubted (e.g. "The preview is rendered at the exact size used on your profile").
    • Rung 2 (Cut Label-Restating Captions): If a field is labeled "Retry limit", delete the caption "How many times to retry".
    • Rung 3 (Cut Bug-Fix Scar Tissue & Permission Slips): Delete captions that rule out past bugs or give polite permission ("Don't worry if the count seems off for a moment", "Feel free to click below").
    • Rung 4 (Delete the DOM Element Entirely): Rather than rewording useless hints, delete the helper container from the DOM. Keep only irreversible consequences, invisible compatibility scopes, empty-state next actions, and bare units.

Under the Hood: Mathematical Formula & Verifier SourceOptional Deep Dive

The verifier tracks badge text across sibling card groups, compares heading text against adjacent pills, and evaluates content stem overlap:

Garnish Repetition Threshold: No identical badge text on > 25% of sibling cards ($N \ge 3$).
Heading Tautology: Case-insensitive word overlap between heading <h1..h4> and adjacent status pill < 1.
Zero-Bit Copy Slop: Label-to-caption content stem overlap $< 55%$, zero hedging/scar-tissue tokens.

What to Look For & How the Linter Fixes It

Visual signs in your UI and how the verifier guides your coding agent.

Visual Symptom
  • Every single card in a table or grid displays an identical 'Active' or 'Success' badge.
  • A card with the heading 'Active Nodes' has a badge right next to it saying 'Active'.
  • Helper captions merely restate field labels (e.g. 'Retry limit' followed by 'How many times to retry').
  • Defensive scar-tissue phrases clutter cards ('Don't worry if it looks slow', 'Feel free to click below').
Linter Check
  • Identical badge text repeats across > 25% of sibling cards.
  • Word overlap between heading text and adjacent status pill.
  • Label-caption content stem overlap >= 55% or defensive hedging phrases.
